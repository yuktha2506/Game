# ⭐ Star Friends: Magical Island Adventure ⭐

An adorable, vibrant, and engaging 2D platformer & pet-rescue adventure designed specifically for kids!

---

## 🎮 How to Play

No installation or downloads needed!
Simply double-click **`index.html`** or open it in any modern web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Mozilla Firefox, Safari, or mobile/tablet browsers).

---

## 🌟 Game Highlights

- **✨ 3 Adorable Playable Heroes**:
  - 🐰 **Pip the Bunny**: Fluffy ears that flutter, with an extra-high flutter jump!
  - 🐱 **Mochi the Cat**: Cute swishing tail, with a swift rainbow dash!
  - 🐲 **Sparky the Baby Dragon**: Flapping mini-wings, with a gentle float glide!
- **🎨 Dress Up & Customizer**:
  - 5 vibrant color palettes: *Blush Pink*, *Sky Blue*, *Mint Green*, *Sunshine Gold*, and *Magic Purple*.
  - Fun hats & accessories: *Party Cone 🎉*, *Royal Crown 👑*, *Superhero Cape 🦸*, *Daisy Flower 🌸*, *Chef Hat 👨‍🍳*, and *Star Antenna 🌟*.
- **🏝️ 3 Handcrafted Worlds**:
  - 🍬 **World 1: Candy Meadow**: Bouncing strawberry jelly mushrooms, candy biscuit bridges, and rescue Baby Barnaby the Bunny!
  - ☁️ **World 2: Cloud Kingdom**: Fluffy clouds, rainbow bridges, bubble elevators, and rescue Pippin the Chick!
  - 🌌 **World 3: Cosmic Cove**: Twinkling stars, neon crystal platforms, low-gravity jump pads, and rescue Stella the Star!
- **💖 Cheerful Companion Parade**:
  - Find the golden keys to unlock magic cages and rescue baby animal companions.
  - Rescued friends follow you in a cute, playful hopping parade!
- **📖 Sticker Album & Trophies**:
  - Collect stars and complete islands to unlock shiny reward stickers.
  - Progress is automatically saved in your browser!
- **🎵 100% Procedural Sound & Music**:
  - Real-time Web Audio API synthesizer: musical pentatonic star chime arpeggios, bubbly jump boings, fanfare celebrations, and joyful upbeat background music.
  - Mute/Unmute audio button anytime.
- **🛡️ Kid-Friendly Design**:
  - Zero punishing game overs! If a player drops into a pit, a friendly glowing fairy bubble gently floats them back to safety.
  - Forgiving jump mechanics (coyote time and jump buffering).

## 🕹️ Controls

### Keyboard (Desktop / Laptop)
| Action | Key(s) |
|---|---|
| **Move Left / Right** | `←` / `→` or `A` / `D` |
| **Jump / Double Jump** | `↑` / `W` or `Spacebar` |
| **Rainbow Dash / Glide** | `Shift` or `X` or `C` |
| **Pause Game** | `Esc` or `P` |

### Touch & Tablet
- On-screen touch buttons:
  - `◀` `▶` for movement
  - `🦘` for big bouncy jumps
  - `⚡` for rainbow dash
  - On-screen pause and sound toggles
